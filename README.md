# AI-Assisted Commercial Intelligence Portfolio

I am building a portfolio of practical business applications designed to support different stages of the commercial development lifecycle.

The objective is not to build technology for technology's sake, but to use AI, data and structured commercial frameworks to improve business decisions.

The portfolio currently focuses on four core areas:

### 1. Opportunity Identification

Which leads and accounts deserve commercial attention?

### 2. Commercial Engagement

How should teams engage, follow up and prioritize those opportunities?

### 3. Strategic Partnerships

Which organizations, partners and ecosystems offer the strongest strategic fit?

### 4. International Market Expansion

Which markets should organizations enter, and what should the commercial entry strategy look like?

Together, these applications form a practical commercial intelligence toolkit for Business Development, Partnerships, GTM and international growth teams.
