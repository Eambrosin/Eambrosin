# 90-Second Commercial Intelligence Demo Script

## Video Title

**From Market Discovery to Qualification-First Outreach in 90 Seconds**

## Goal

Show that the portfolio is a Business Development operating workflow, not a collection of disconnected apps.

## Script

### 0–10 sec — Positioning

> I am an International Business Development professional, and I built this Commercial Intelligence workflow to make account discovery, qualification and outreach more structured and explainable.

Show the GitHub profile / funnel:

```text
IDENTIFY → PRIORITIZE → ENGAGE
          PARTNER
```

### 10–30 sec — IDENTIFY

> Here I configure the target market and run public-web discovery. Instead of treating every search result as a lead, the system separates real target accounts from research, vendors and weak results.

Show:
- Discovery Executive Summary
- Target Account Ranking

### 30–50 sec — Evidence & qualification

> For a selected account, I separate opportunity attractiveness from research completeness. The workflow enriches the account, validates public evidence and keeps unknown information explicit.

Show:
- Account Intelligence Workspace
- Qualification Readiness
- Account Enrichment

### 50–65 sec — Contact validation

> The contact layer uses public indexed evidence, role and location to identify a plausible profile and hold back weak homonyms.

Show:
- LinkedIn Validation shortlist

### 65–82 sec — ENGAGE

> The account then moves to ENGAGE with its evidence, qualification questions and open gaps. The system chooses an available public channel and generates a qualification-first sequence without exposing internal scores or inventing buying intent.

Show:
- Upstream Qualification Intelligence
- Primary Channel
- generated outreach sequence

### 82–90 sec — Close

> The point is not AI for its own sake. It is to preserve commercial judgment, evidence and next actions from market discovery through outreach.

End on:

**International Business Development | Strategic Partnerships | GTM | Commercial Intelligence**

## Recording Notes

- keep the browser zoom at a readable level
- remove unrelated browser tabs
- use one real demonstration account already present in the portfolio
- do not expose API keys or private client data
- avoid explaining every feature; demonstrate the decision flow
- aim for 75–100 seconds rather than stretching beyond two minutes
