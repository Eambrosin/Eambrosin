# LinkedIn Case Post Draft

## Suggested Opening

**A list of 200 prospects is not yet a sales strategy.**

One of the problems I have been working on is how to turn public market research into a commercial queue that a Business Development team can actually use.

The question was simple:

> How do we move from “these companies exist” to “this account is worth approaching, here is the evidence, here is what we still do not know, and here is the next commercial action”?

## Case

I used a Medical Aesthetics territory in Northern Italy as a practical demonstration.

The workflow now does five things:

1. **IDENTIFY** — discovers potential accounts from public-web research and separates real account candidates from directories, vendors and research-only results.
2. **ASSESS** — calculates Account Opportunity while keeping research completeness separate through Qualification Readiness.
3. **ENRICH** — validates direct website, location, public contact paths and observed treatment / technology evidence.
4. **VALIDATE CONTACT** — uses public LinkedIn evidence, professional role and location to reduce homonym risk.
5. **ENGAGE** — hands the evidence downstream and generates a qualification-first outreach sequence using an actually observed public channel.

What I find most important is what the system **does not** do.

It does not treat a high score as purchase intent.

It does not turn missing information into fact.

It does not assume that a LinkedIn homonym is the right decision-maker.

And it does not let the AI decide which account deserves priority.

The scoring and commercial logic remain deterministic and explainable. AI, when used, assists research and communication after the commercial decision layer.

## Closing

For me, this is the useful intersection between **Business Development and AI**:

**commercial experience provides the judgment; structured data provides consistency; technology provides leverage.**
